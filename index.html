Privacy Policy — Depth Runner

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains how Depth Runner ("the App"), developed by Rolling Byte Studio ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to play. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device:

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data such as your advertising ID, device information, approximate location, IP address, and app usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, and to measure ad performance.

You can learn more about how Google collects and processes data in this context here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization).

3. Analytics and Leaderboard (Google Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase to store leaderboard scores and, where enabled, basic anonymous usage analytics to help us understand how the App is used and improve it. This may include your chosen display name (if you enter one for the leaderboard), your score, and general device/usage information. Firebase's data handling practices are described at Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not require account creation, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, or photos.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. If the App is configured for a child-directed audience on a given store listing, ad requests will be made using Google's child-directed treatment settings, which limit data collection and personalized advertising accordingly.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data described above is processed by our service providers (Google AdMob, Google Firebase) under their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads via your device's ad settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time to stop all data collection associated with it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us
